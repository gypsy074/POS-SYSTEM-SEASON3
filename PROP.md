## 1. Executive Summary

Season 3 Cafe deserves a checkout that keeps up with the lunch rush. This
proposal presents a complete **Point-of-Sale (POS) system** — a cashier
counter that runs on any phone, tablet, or computer, plus an owner's panel
that shows exactly what the cafe earns, sells, wastes, and needs to restock —
all in real time.

Four things it delivers from day one:

1. **Faster checkout** — tap items, see the change instantly, swipe to pay.
2. **No lost sales when the internet drops** — orders placed offline
   auto-sync when the connection returns.
3. **Clear numbers, every day** — revenue, orders, top-selling items, and a
   forecast of tomorrow's sales.
4. **Full control and accountability** — staff roles, a record of every
   action, and the ability to log out any device remotely.

## 3. What You Get

### Sales & Reports
- **Live dashboard** — today's revenue, orders sold, top item, and waste cost
  at a glance
- **Sales analytics** — charts by day, week, month, or all time; a calendar
  you can click to see any day; CSV export for Excel
- **AI forecast** — a prediction of tomorrow's expected sales (with a low–high
  range) based on the last 7 days
- **Restock alerts** — the system flags items running low and suggests how
  many to order
- **Anomaly warnings** — it notices unusually bad (or good) days, like a
  sudden revenue drop, so you can find out why
- **Weekly AI business report** — one button generates a plain-language
  summary of the week's performance

### Cashier Experience
- **Touch-friendly ordering** — tap to add items, adjust quantities, remove
- **Payment calculator** — pick a bill amount, the change is computed and
  broken down instantly (quick ₱20/₱50/₱100 buttons)
- **Swipe-to-pay** — the signature move: swipe right to complete the sale
- **Printed receipts** — every order prints a receipt
- **Offline mode** — if the internet drops mid-rush, the cashier keeps
  selling; orders queue up and sync automatically when the connection
  returns. No order is ever lost or doubled.

### Stock & Waste
- **Menu manager** — add, edit, or remove items with photos; sold-out items
  are flagged automatically
- **Separate stock counts** — menu stock counts sellable portions; Inventory
  tracks ingredients and supplies such as beans, syrups, and cups
- **Linked supply tracking** — link a supply to a menu item and set the
  quantity consumed per sale; menu portions and supply quantities remain
  separate, and either can be restocked on its own
- **Low-stock alerts** — the cashier's bell notifies when menu items run low
- **Waste logging** — one-tap food waste entry; its value feeds the reports
  and restock suggestions

### Security & Control
- **Two roles** — Admin (full control) and Cashier (ring up orders)
- **Account control** — see every device logged in, and log out any device
  remotely (great when a staff phone is lost or a staff member leaves)
- **Audit log** — a dated record of logins, voids, password changes, and
  account changes
- **Protected access** — logins are rate-limited and passwords are required
  to be at least 8 characters

## 4. How It Works Day to Day

**The cashier (30 seconds):**
1. Tap the items on the screen
2. Enter the cash received (or pick a quick bill amount)
3. Swipe to pay → receipt prints → order is recorded

**The owner (end of day):**
1. Open the dashboard — today's revenue and orders are already there
2. Check the waste log and the restock list
3. One click exports the day to Excel
4. Once a week: press "Generate report" for the AI business summary

**No setup burden:** it runs in the browser on any device — no apps to
install, no server in the cafe, no updates to run manually.
